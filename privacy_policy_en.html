WristNoti Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026 · Developer: JBMSOFT · Contact: jbmsoft.help@gmail.com

Summary: WristNoti does NOT collect, upload, store, or sell your notification contents, messages, contacts, or quick replies to any external server. All processing happens strictly locally on your smartphone and your paired Wear OS smartwatch.

1. Data Accessed and Purpose

  • Notification Access (NotificationListenerService): With your explicit consent, WristNoti reads incoming notification app labels, senders, conversation titles, message text, optional preview thumbnails (Pro), and reply actions solely to sync alert history, filter ad buzzes, generate on-device group digests, trigger custom watch haptics, and send quick replies.
  • Preferences: Quiet hours, per-app rules, and quick-reply templates are synced strictly between your phone and paired watch using Google Play Services Wearable Data Layer.
  • In-App Purchases: Handled exclusively by Google Play Billing to verify Pro entitlement.

2. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

WristNoti contains no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics trackers, and no external cloud servers. Your notification data is never shared with or sold to third parties.

3. Retention & Deletion

  • Alert logs reside locally in your device database (rolling window of up to 100 entries on Free, or 2,000+ on Pro).
  • You can delete all stored alerts at any time via Settings > Clear All Recorded History, revoke Notification Access in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.

4. Contact

If you have questions about privacy or data handling, contact: jbmsoft.help@gmail.com
